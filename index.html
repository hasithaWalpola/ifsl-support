01

Check your A/L year

The 2026/27 handbook covers Sri Lankan G.C.E. A/L candidates from 2023, 2024, or 2025 who qualify for university entrance.

02

Check general requirements

Three S passes in one sitting, within three sittings; at least 30 for the Common General Test within three sittings; an S pass in A/L General English or O/L English Language; and age 25 or below on 27 September 2026.

03

Check your chosen degree

Every degree has its own entry requirements. Selection also depends on preferences, available seats and, when oversubscribed, A/L Z-score.

Requirements can change by intake. Confirm the current notice and the exact degree requirements in the official handbook before submitting.

THE APPLICATION

From registration to interview

Keep the same email address throughout your studies.
  1. 01

    Register on the official portal

    Use the applicant's NIC number used for A/L and the relevant A/L index number. The handbook says your password is sent to your email.

    Go to official portal ↗
  2. 02

    Read the handbook, then complete the form

    Enter your details carefully, review degree-specific requirements and preferences, and submit the online application before the intake deadline.

    Open the English handbook ↗
  3. 03

    Save and print your submitted application

    Keep a copy of what you submitted. For an interview, sign the printed application and complete the school-candidate certificate or external-candidate affidavit on its last page.

  4. 04

    Watch for your interview invitation

    The handbook says interview calls are sent by email and published on the Ministry website. Your own invitation gives the date, time and Microsoft Teams link.

  5. 05

    Email your scans before the interview

    Follow your invitation. The email shared for this guide asks for the named scans to be sent one hour before the interview.

Make a PDF from your photos

  1. Choose the document you want to prepare.
  2. Tap “Choose photos” and select one or more clear pictures.
  3. Tap “Create PDF”. The named file downloads to your device.
